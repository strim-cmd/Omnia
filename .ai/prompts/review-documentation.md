- Is the document written in English and Markdown?
- Does it have a clear purpose?
- Does it contain the required sections: Purpose, Scope, Requirements, Non-Goals, Related Documents?

- Does it contradict any existing documentation?

- Is the writing concise and production-ready?

- Suggest a concrete fix for each issue.
