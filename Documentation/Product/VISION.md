# Our Vision

Build the best native AI workspace for Apple platforms.

Not the biggest.

Not the most feature-rich.

The best.

---

# Why Omnia Exists

Artificial intelligence should not be tied to a single provider.

Users deserve freedom.

They should be able to choose:

- their provider
- their infrastructure
- their models
- their workflow

without changing applications.

Omnia exists to make that possible.

---

# Mission

Create a beautiful, privacy-first, native application that becomes the universal interface for OpenAI-compatible AI services.

Omnia never owns the AI.

The user does.

---

# Product Principles

The Product Principles are the single source of truth for how product decisions are made. They are defined in `Documentation/Product/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`, and every feature and product decision must be consistent with them:

- User Ownership
- Privacy First
- Native Experience
- Simplicity Wins
- Provider Independence
- Performance Matters
- Documentation First
- Long-Term Thinking

---

# Success

We will consider Omnia successful if:

- users trust it
- developers enjoy contributing
- new providers can be integrated easily
- the codebase remains understandable after years of development
